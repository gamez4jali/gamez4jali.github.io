Tap Cricket Go! - Privacy Policy

Android package com.g4jali.tapcricketgo · Developer: Gamez4Jali · Last updated: 28 October 2026

In short: your game progress stays on your phone. The game shows ads through Google AdMob (with a consent form where the law requires it), offers one optional purchase ("Remove Ads") through Google Play, and signs you in to Google Play Games for achievements and leaderboards. We never ask for your name, email or phone number, and we never sell your information.

This policy explains what information the mobile game Tap Cricket Go! ("the game") uses, why, and the choices you have. The game is published on Google Play by Gamez4Jali ("we").

1. Information stored on your device

Your game progress (club name and crest, players and the names you give them, in-game cash, trophies, Cricket Pass, achievements, settings, and whether you bought "Remove Ads") is saved only on your phone so the game works offline. It is not sent to our servers. It is removed when you uninstall the game or clear its storage.

2. Google Play Games

When you open the game, Google Play Games may sign you in automatically. Google shares your Play Games player ID and gamer name with the game, and the game sends your achievements and your leaderboard scores (best score, trophies and career boundaries) to Google Play Games, where other players can see them on the leaderboards. Google Play Games is operated by Google under the Google Privacy Policy. You can manage or delete your Play Games profile and game data in the Google Play Games app.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows banner ads, occasional full-screen ads between matches, and optional reward videos (for example to double your match cash or to unlock a player card). To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google AdMob may collect and process your device's advertising ID, your IP address and approximate location derived from it, device information (such as model, operating system and language) and information about your interactions with ads.

In the European Economic Area, the UK, Switzerland and where otherwise required, the game asks for your consent using Google's consent form before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy settings. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Android settings. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

4. In-app purchases (Google Play)

The game offers an optional one-time purchase, Remove Ads, which removes banner and full-screen ads (optional reward videos stay available). Payments are processed entirely by Google Play; we never receive your payment details. Google Play tells the game only which items you own, so a purchase can be restored with Settings → Restore purchases. The game uses Unity In-App Purchasing to talk to Google Play, which may process a random installation identifier and technical purchase information.

5. Crash and performance information

The game is built with the Unity engine. Unity may automatically collect limited technical information when the game crashes or has a performance problem (for example device model, operating system version and the error report). This does not identify you and is used only to keep the game stable. See Unity's Privacy Policy.

6. Ratings

After a win, the game may show Google Play's built-in rating window. Any rating or review you leave is handled by Google; we never see who rated the game.

8. Your choices and deleting your data

  • Ads consent: Settings → Privacy settings (where shown), and your phone's advertising ID settings.
  • No ads: buy Remove Ads in Settings.
  • Game data: uninstall the game or clear its storage to delete everything stored on your phone.
  • Play Games data: delete it in the Google Play Games app (Settings → Delete Play Games account and data).
  • Anything else: email us (see how to delete your data); we reply within 30 days.

9. Children

Tap Cricket Go! is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Security and international transfers

Information sent by the game travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Google and Unity may process it in countries other than yours, under their own safeguards.

11. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU/UK or the CCPA in California), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or object to the use of your information. Contact us and we will respond within 30 days.

12. Changes

We may update this policy when the game changes. The date at the top shows the latest version.
