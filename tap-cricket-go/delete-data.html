Tap Cricket Go! - Delete your data

Developer: Gamez4Jali

Tap Cricket Go! has no online account of its own: your progress is stored only on your phone.

Game progress on your phone

  1. Uninstall the game, or
  2. Open your phone's Settings → Apps → Tap Cricket Go! → Storage → Clear storage.

This permanently deletes your club, players, cash, trophies and settings on that phone.

Google Play Games achievements and scores

Open the Google Play Games app → your profile → Settings → Delete Play Games account and data, or manage it at myaccount.google.com.

Advertising ID

Reset or delete it in your phone's Settings → Privacy → Ads (or Settings → Google → Ads).

By email

For anything else, email gamez4jali.support@gmail.com with the subject "Tap Cricket Go data deletion". We reply within 30 days.

What is kept

  • Purchase records held by Google Play (Google keeps these under its own policy; "Remove Ads" can still be restored).
