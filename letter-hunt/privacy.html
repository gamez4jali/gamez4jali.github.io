Letter Hunt - Privacy Policy

Developer: Gamez4Jali · Last updated: 31 October 2026

This policy explains how the mobile game Letter Hunt: Word Puzzle ("the game", Android package com.g4jali.letterhunt) handles information. The game is published on Google Play by Gamez4Jali ("we").

1. In short

The game does not collect, store on our servers or share any personal information. There is no sign-up, no advertising and no in-app purchases, and the game works fully offline.

2. Information stored on your device

Your game progress (levels, stars, gold, power-ups, streaks, word album, trophies, themes and settings such as sound) is saved only on your phone so the game can remember where you are. It is never sent to us. It is removed when you uninstall the game or clear the game's storage in your phone's settings.

3. What the game does not do

  • No advertising and no advertising ID.
  • No accounts, sign-in, names, email addresses or other contact details.
  • No analytics or tracking across apps or websites.
  • No access to your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.
  • No in-app purchases; gold is earned by playing.
  • We never sell or share your information.

4. Google Play

You download the game from Google Play, which is operated by Google under the Google Privacy Policy. Google may provide us with aggregated, anonymous statistics (such as install counts and crash rates) through the Google Play Console; these do not identify you.

5. Children

Letter Hunt is a family-friendly game and follows the Google Play Families Policy. Because the game does not collect personal information from anyone, it does not collect personal information from children.

6. Deleting your data

All game data lives on your phone. To delete it, uninstall the game or open your phone's Settings → Apps → Letter Hunt → Storage → Clear data. We hold no data about you to delete.

7. Changes

If the game changes how it handles information, we will update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
