Harvest Twist - Delete your account and data

Developer: Gamez4Jali

In the game (fastest)

  1. Open Harvest Twist and tap the gear (Settings).
  2. Tap Delete my data, then Delete to confirm.

This immediately deletes your cloud save, your club membership and your online account, and resets the game on that phone.

Without the game

Email gamez4jali.support@gmail.com with the subject "Harvest Twist data deletion" and the farm name shown in Settings (for example "Lucky Sower 408"). We delete your data within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

  • Your cloud save (game progress, coins, items, purchase records kept by the game).
  • Your club membership, generated farm name and club score.
  • Your game account identifier (Firebase Authentication).

What is kept

  • Purchase records held by Google Play (Google keeps these under its own policy).
  • Aggregated, pseudonymous analytics and crash reports, deleted automatically within 14 months.
